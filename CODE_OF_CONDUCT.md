# Code of Conduct

Ops by Agent is a technical learning and open-source engineering space. We aim to make participation respectful, constructive and welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, professional and constructive.
- Critique technical ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Explain disagreements with specific reasoning and reproducible facts.
- Accept corrections and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Keep discussions focused and useful to other readers.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities
Maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method on the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces operated by the maintainers.
